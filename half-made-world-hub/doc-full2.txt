[47] The Sundering Altar
[48] At 15, every person in the world is brought to The Sundering Altar, where their latent connection to Veyn is unlocked for the first time. Before this, no one — human, elf, angel, demon — can consciously access magic. The altar doesn't teach them to use it; it simply opens the channel.
[49] The moment Veyn awakens in them, it manifests visibly in a way that reveals their affinity — the specific path/discipline they are suited to (e.g., combat-magic, healing, elemental control, beast-bonding, etc.). This is not a choice the person makes consciously in the moment; it is revealed to them, based on what's already latent inside them — which is why it works well as "awakening" rather than "selection." The path-choosing comes after once someone knows their affinity, they then choose how to pursue it (which order, mentor, discipline, or role to commit to).

[81] Humans
[82] Unlike other races, humans aren't locked into one Veyn affinity for life. When an elf, angel, or demon is revealed at the altar, that affinity stays fixed forever, but a human's affinity can change or grow over time through training. This means a human could eventually learn more than one affinity, something no other race can do.
[83] The catch is that switching or adding a new affinity is extremely hard. Most humans try and give up, choosing to stick with the one affinity they got at the altar instead. Even if the ones who succeed never become as powerful in any single affinity as a race built specifically for it, they trade raw power for versatility.
[84] Veyn
[85] It is what allows people to use magic in the world. It is drawn from the core of the world and everything around us. Veyn naturally glows a pale silver-blue, like faint moonlight. This is its raw, neutral form before it's shaped into something else, holy energy brightens it into gold, while corrupted Veyn (like a demon's miasma) darkens it into deep purple or black.
[86] Ren
[87] It is what swordsmen use in place for Veyn. It is the inner energy from our body brought out of our body to enhance our strength. Ren glows a deep crimson red since it is drawn from a person's own Vitality rather than an outside source. Where Veyn feels cool and ambient, Ren feels warm and physical, more like a pulse than a light.
[88] Healer
[89] There are only seven healers in the Human Realm. When one dies, a new healer is chosen within the next two years. One person in the Human Realm knows who the next healer will be; this role is called The Seer. The Seer is entrusted with a gem known as The Truthstone which has the power to locate who the new healer is. The Seer is the only one who knows the gem’s location.
[90] Fallen Angel
[91] They have black spotted white wings or black cherry spotted white wings and a broken halo on top of their head.
[92] Once the vow breaks, the angel is cast out of the Angel Realm and rejected by their own race, this is what makes them a Fallen Angel. They keep their wings and whatever power they had, but can never take another Veyn Vow again, so their connection to Veyn stays unstable and unregulated. This makes them strong in bursts but dangerous even to themselves since nothing filters how much Veyn moves through them anymore.
[93] The Angel Veyn Vow
[94] Every angel takes a Veyn Vow when their halo first forms, it binds their access to Veyn to their obedience to the angelic hierarchy. It's not just spoken, it's a magical bond, the halo is the physical seal of it. If the vow holds, an angel can freely use Veyn within their rank's limit, and the halo regulates it, so it doesn't overwhelm them.
[95] The vow is the reason angels can hold as much power as they do, without it their body can't safely filter raw Veyn on its own. Rank affects the vow too, a higher rank means a stronger vow and halo, so they can channel more Veyn without breaking.
[96] An Angel Breaking the vow.
[97] The vow breaks when an angel disobeys someone above their rank or pulls more Veyn than their vow allows to act against an order. Once it breaks the halo can't hold the channel steady and shatters, becoming the broken halo Fallen Angels are known for.
[98] The wings get affected too. The uncontrolled Veyn surging through them at that moment leaves permanent black spotting on the feathers, some end up with plain black spots, others with black cherry spotting depending on how much Veyn surged through when it broke.

[131a] Standard Scope: Standard demon Soul Sight ONLY perceives the soul's movement — predicting the direction and timing of physical attacks. [131b] MC Enhancement: The MC's Soul Sight is significantly more powerful due to his angel half. His enhanced version perceives: (1) Soul movement for attack prediction, (2) Corruption spreading in real time, (3) Spiritual nature of beings, (4) Vireth's influence reaching toward him like a hand. He watches the sword's corruption try to bend his will and actively fights it off through vigilance.
[131c] Demon vs MC Experience: DEMON WIELDING VIRETH — Soul Sight predicts attacks but CANNOT see corruption. They experience: whispering grows louder, moral compass blurs, veins darken, aura shifts, blackouts occur, eventually full possession. They fight blind — stronger in combat but losing themselves. Jaiden Wielding Vireth — Soul Sight lets him see corruption approaching in real time. He watches the sword's influence like watching a hand reach for his throat. He can resist because he can see it coming. He keeps the other eyes closed through vigilance, not strength.
[131d] Vireth Counter-Mechanics: Each eye opening = corruption deepening. Jaiden's Soul Sight counters by: (1) Seeing the sword's whispers before they take hold, (2) Watching his moral compass blur and actively resisting, (3) Observing corruption spread through his veins and fighting it, (4) Detecting the sword attempting to override his will. Without Soul Sight, a wielder experiences these effects passively — they happen TO them. With Soul Sight, Jaiden experiences them actively — he watches and chooses to resist.
[131e] Why Demons Cannot Survive Vireth: Even if a demon possessed Soul Sight, they cannot survive Vireth because: (1) Soul Sight only predicts physical attacks, not corruption, (2) The sword's divine nature counters demonic regeneration, (3) Corruption overwhelms demon souls faster than angel-touched souls, (4) Without Jaiden's angelic resistance, the sword's whispers eventually break any will. The MC's angel half provides the spiritual anchor that lets him resist what would consume any demon.

The World Barrier
The world barrier was raised to split the realms apart after the old race's tearing-open of the Hidden Realm nearly destroyed the world. Its magic is drawn directly from the untouched world core — the same primal source from which Veyn is pulled — rather than from any single caster, which is why it has endured without ever being re-made. The Shudon, stone golems carved from the world core, were set at the entrances to the three main realms — Human, Angel, and Demon — to guard the barrier and keep the world from collapsing inward. The Hidden Realm has no Shudon; its only entrance is the wandering portal.

The Sixth Sense Ranking System
Every person in the world is ranked on a scale based on how much Veyn they can channel and how effectively they can use it. The ranks from lowest to highest are:
F Grade → E Grade → D Grade → C Grade → B Grade → A Grade → S Grade
Each grade has sub-stages: Early, Mid, Late, Peak.
Note: The amount of Veyn someone can use from birth is already set and if you want to increase it then finish it and keep replenishing your Veyn for it to increase.

Rune Star Circle
A system used to measure the depth of a person's Veyn channels — how much raw magical energy they can hold and circulate at once. The circles range from 1st Circle (weakest) to 6th Circle (strongest). A higher circle means more Veyn can be channeled simultaneously, allowing for more powerful spells and techniques.

New Monsters (added to the world)

Werewolves
Humans or humanoids cursed by corrupted Veyn exposure during a full moon, forced into a bestial wolf form they cannot control. In their shifted form they are faster and stronger than any natural wolf, with claws that can rend steel and a bite that injects corrupted Veyn into the victim, beginning the corruption sickness. They retain fragments of their human mind — enough to recognize loved ones but never enough to stop themselves. The curse cannot be removed by conventional healing; only a high-rank Exorcist's Purification or a Saintess's Holy Purge can purge the corrupted Veyn from their blood. A werewolf that has transformed more than seven times is considered past the point of no return.
Location: Thornlost Forest (Hidden Realm); occasionally near border zones of the Umbrage Forest

Basilisk
Massive serpents up to forty feet long with scales of dark iron-grey that shimmer with an oily sheen. Their most dangerous feature is their gaze — prolonged eye contact petrifies living flesh. The petrification is not instant death; victims remain conscious as their body turns to stone. The stone is corrupted Veyn crystallized around the victim's body, which means an Exorcist's Purify can reverse the early stages but not the advanced ones. Basilisk eyes, once removed and properly preserved, are a component in powerful defensive enchantments.
Location: Deep Umbrage Forest; Thornlost Forest darker reaches
Weakness: They cannot petrify through reflective surfaces; mirrors or polished shields bounce the gaze back.

Manticore
A terrifying hybrid with the body of a massive lion, bat-like wings that allow short bursts of flight, and a segmented scorpion tail tipped with a barbed stinger loaded with paralytic venom. Manticores are solitary hunters that rely on ambush. Their roar is unnaturally loud and can be heard for miles. A Manticore's venom sac is one of the most valuable alchemical ingredients in the world — it is the base component for Paralytic Venom.
Location: Umbrage Forest; occasionally deep Arubian Forest

Chimera
A monstrous hybrid with three distinct heads — a lion's head that roars with concussive force, a goat's head that breathes corrosive bile, and a serpent's head that strikes with venom far more potent than a Basilisk's. The three heads operate semi-independently. Its blood is flammable when exposed to air. Chimera blood is highly prized by Alchemists for creating Explosive Mixing compounds.
Location: Deep Umbrage Forest; Thornlost Forest (Hidden Realm)
Weakness: The three heads sometimes disagree on targeting, creating brief windows of incoordination.

Cockatrice
A smaller relative of the Basilisk, but far more cunning. The Cockatrice has the body of a rooster covered in dull bronze scales, small dragon-like wings that allow gliding, and a hooked beak that secretes a thin layer of petrifying residue through physical contact. Cockatrices hunt in mated pairs. Their petrifying residue is used by Enchanters to create Wardseal-class defensive runes.
Location: Umbrage Forest borders; Thornlost Forest edges

Goblins
Small, wiry humanoids standing barely three feet tall, with mottled green-grey skin, oversized ears that give them exceptional hearing, and yellow eyes adapted for near-total darkness. They travel in warrens of fifty to several hundred. Their true strength lies in their intelligence and their obsessive crafting of traps. They fear fire above all else.
Location: Umbrage Forest; deeper Arubian Forest

Orcs
Massive humanoids standing between six and eight feet tall, with thick grey-green skin, protruding tusks, and dense musculature. They have a functioning society with ranks, customs, and a brutal form of honor. They live in loosely organized war-bands led by the strongest individual. Orcs do not use magic in the traditional sense, but their bodies naturally channel a crude form of Ren through sheer physical will.
Location: Umbrage Forest; border regions of the Demon Realm

Abyssal Wyrm
A creature so old it predates the division of the three realms — an entity that feeds on Veyn at a catastrophic scale, draining entire regions of ambient magic until the land itself begins to die. It was sealed beneath the world's crust by the Aelthar during their reign. Its breath does not burn or freeze; it nullifies all Veyn in its path, creating zones of absolute magic silence. It can only be harmed by weapons forged from world-core material (Shudon-grade metal). The seal is weakening.
Location: Sealed beneath the world's crust

World Eater
Not a creature born of Veyn but something that exists outside it — a parasitic consciousness that embeds itself in the world core and slowly drains its power over centuries. It manifests as a spreading darkness within the core's energy, a corruption that converts pure world-core Veyn into null — an absence of magical energy. The Aelthar discovered its existence and it was one of the reasons they created the Hidden Realm. The Shudon were originally built not just as realm guardians but as core regulators, designed to detect and suppress the World Eater's spread.
Location: Embedded within the world core

Void Colossus
A towering entity of unknown origin that exists in the spaces between realms — in the void that separates the four Realms from one another. It is not alive in any conventional sense. It moves through the void with purpose, and that purpose appears to be finding and destroying breaches in the realm barrier. It manifests at the breach point as a towering humanoid figure of compressed void energy, thirty to fifty feet tall. It attacks anything near the breach with sweeps of its arms that create localized spatial distortions. It cannot be killed because it is not truly present in any realm; it is a projection of the void's own immune response.
Location: The void between realms; manifests at barrier breaches
Weakness: Closing the breach it was drawn to forces it to withdraw.

Storm Titan
A being formed from centuries of accumulated elemental Veyn concentrated in a single location — a living storm given form and awareness. Storm Titans appear as humanoid figures made of swirling wind, rain, and lightning, standing between twenty and thirty feet tall. They are not born; they coalesce. A Storm Titan can control weather within a ten-mile radius of its body with absolute precision. They can be dispersed by sufficient magical force, but the elemental Veyn that formed them simply scatters and will re-coalesce over decades.
Location: Regions with sustained severe weather

Bloodwraith Sovereign
The most powerful wraith-class spirit in existence — an entire consciousness that has fed on the blood and Veyn of thousands of victims over centuries. Its touch drains vitality directly, aging the victim's body decades in seconds. It can only be truly destroyed by a combination of holy purification and Exorcist-class spiritual banishment. If destroyed, the thousands of consumed wills it carries are released simultaneously, creating a shockwave of spiritual energy that can drive weak-willed individuals insane.
Location: Battlefields, massacre sites, plague pits

Skywhales
Massive creatures, sometimes hundreds of feet long, that float through the upper atmosphere on currents of concentrated Veyn. Their skin is a translucent pale blue that glows faintly in moonlight. Skywhales are completely passive; they filter-feed on ambient Veyn. Skywhale blubber is one of the most valuable alchemical ingredients in the world. The Pallantine Elves skyride on Griffens to carefully harvest blubber under strict quotas.
Location: Upper atmosphere of all three realms

Lightwisps
Small, floating orbs of warm golden-white light that appear to lost or endangered travelers. They guide them toward safety with patient, persistent hovering. They are formed from naturally occurring Veyn exposed to so much ambient positive intent that it has developed rudimentary pattern-matching behavior. They cannot be captured or controlled.
Location: All three realms

Stonetenders
Slow-moving humanoid figures made of compacted earth, stone, and root systems. Their sole function is land maintenance: they smooth erosion, fill sinkholes, break up dangerous rockfalls. They appear in regions where the land has been damaged. They cannot be tamed or commanded.
Location: Regions with land damage — post-battlefields, earthquake zones, magical disruption sites

Dreamweavers
Moth-like creatures the size of a human hand, with translucent wings that shimmer with faint Veyn traces. They are drawn to individuals suffering from psychological trauma and enter the person's dream to stabilize the emotional landscape. The process is gradual — repeated visits over weeks can resolve deep-seated trauma. They cannot cure magical corruption or physical illness.
Location: Arubian Forest; around the Sundering Altar

Stonehollow Elk
Massive elk-like creatures standing twelve feet at the shoulder, with antlers that are crystallized Veyn. When shed naturally, the antlers remain conductive to Veyn for decades, making them one of the most sought-after materials for Enchanters and Smiths. Their Veyn-rich antlers give them a natural aura that calms hostile creatures. Killing a Stonehollow Elk is considered deeply taboo in Pallantine culture.
Location: Arubian Forest; occasionally Thornlost Forest

Ashbound Spirits
Spirits formed from the residual will and Veyn of people who died in fires that consumed entire settlements. They manifest as flickering humanoid shapes made of embers and ash. They are drawn to fire and heat, becoming agitated and hostile when they detect living beings nearby. They can be dispersed by removing the fire source that anchors them.
Location: Burned settlement sites; near bonfires, forges, and volcanic areas

Tidecallers
Spirits formed from the residual Veyn of the Sentient River — they are extensions of it, manifestations of the river's own awareness. They maintain the river's currents and enforce the river's will — protecting the seal on Vireth, the Fallen Grace. They can be evaded by demonstrating pure intent.
Location: Sentient River (Hidden Realm); any connected water source

Thornweald
Spirits bound to trees that have stood for over a thousand years — the accumulated Veyn those trees have absorbed over centuries, which has developed enough complexity to become aware. They are the forest's immune system: when a tree in their territory is threatened, the Thornweald moves to protect it. They can be permanently killed by destroying the tree they are bound to, but doing so triggers a forest-wide response.
Location: Oldest parts of Arubian Forest, Thornlost Forest, ancient Umbrage groves

Echoes of the Fallen
Spirits formed from the residual Veyn and willpower of warriors who died in major historical battles. They appear as translucent, flickering figures engaged in perpetual combat — they fight, they fall, they rise, and they fight again. They are not hostile to the living; they do not recognize the living at all. For military historians and strategists, Echo battlefields are invaluable resources. The most famous is the field where the Aelthar were wiped out.
Location: Major historical battlefields

Gloomveil Specters
Spirits that form in places where someone has been alone for so long their Veyn has begun to decay. They feed by projecting a field of intense loneliness and hopelessness that deepens the victim's own despair until they give up the will to move, fight, or even call for help. The victim falls into a waking coma of total apathy while the Specter slowly drains their Veyn. They are weak in terms of raw power — any burst of strong willpower can disperse one.
Location: Lethmoor ruins; abandoned Umbrage sections; sealed Academy rooms

Flora and Fauna

Human Realm: Common crops (wheat, barley, potatoes), domesticated animals (horses, cattle, chickens), and Ether Forest edge creatures (Fairies, Pallantine wildlife). The Arubian Forest is rich with Veyn-infused plants — Sunpetal flowers that glow at night, Thornmoss that seals wounds, and Everbloom vines that never die.

Angel Realm: Everlight Trees (golden-glowing memorial trees), Angelic Orchids (flowers that only bloom near high concentrations of Holy Energy), and Harmonic Birds (small songbirds whose calls stabilize ambient Veyn).

Demon Realm: Corruption-resistant plants (Blackthorn, Ironvine), predatory flora (Strangler Root, Bite Blossom), and aggressive fauna (blood-drinking insects, shadow cats).

Hidden Realm: Time-distorted organisms — plants that grow decades in what feels like days (due to time dilation), creatures that exist in multiple time states simultaneously, and the Ebonspire Tree (100-year-old tree that grows near dragon nests).

Ether Forest: The boundary zone contains hybrid organisms — creatures and plants that exhibit both Arubian (light) and Umbrage (dark) characteristics.

Note on Ebonspire Trees: Found near dragon nests in the Hidden Realm. Their bark and leaves are ingredients for the Ethereal Potion and Rejuvenating Elixir.

Note on Kaelroot Trees: Found near dragon nests in the Hidden Realm. A 100-year-old tree. The leaves can be used to make the Rejuvenating Elixir, while the bark when grinded to powder can be used in making the Ethereal Potion.

Resources and Materials

Metals: Iron, copper, tin, and gold are mined from mountain ranges in all three realms. The Human Realm's Ashvein Mountains are the largest mining region. Silver comes from the Angel Realm's Moonpeak range. Gold is rare and found only in deep mines in the Demon Realm's volcanic regions.

Veyn Stones: Crystallized Veyn found in areas of high Veyn density — primarily the Ether Forest, the Hidden Realm, and near the Sundering Altar. Lucent Stones are refined from Veyn Stones through a purification process that requires multiple days of Veyn exposure.

Monster Parts: Bones, scales, blood, venom, and organs from monsters are valuable crafting materials. Troll bones are used for building reinforcement; Dragon scales are used for armor; Manticore venom sacs are used for paralytic poisons; Chimera blood is used for explosive compounds.

Ether Forest Resources: The Arubian Forest produces rare herbs, mushrooms, and plant materials used in Alchemy. Sunpetal flowers, Thornmoss, and Everbloom vines are gathered by Pallantine Elves and sold to human merchants. The Umbrage Forest produces corrupted materials — Blackthorn, Ironvine, and corrupted Veyn crystals.

Alchemy and Smithing

Alchemy: The art of combining Veyn with physical ingredients to create potions, poisons, and explosives.
Brewing Process: An Alchemist draws Veyn into a mortar, pestle, or brewing vessel, then adds physical ingredients in a specific sequence. The Veyn reacts with the ingredients, transforming them into the desired effect. The sequence matters — adding ingredients in the wrong order can produce poison instead of healing, or cause an explosion.

Common Potions:
- Healing Potion (5-10sm): Heals minor to moderate injuries.
- Restoration Potion (10-15gc): Restores Veyn reserves.
- Paralytic Venom (50+gc): Paralyzes targets up to C-rank. Base component: Manticore venom sac.
- Purification Solution (5gc per dose): Cleanses minor Corruption Sickness.
- Explosive Mix (20gc): Creates a blast on impact. Ingredients: Chimera blood, Veyn Stone dust, sulfur.

Advanced Potions:
- Lucent Reserve (50+gc): Full restoration of Veyn or Ren. Requires Lucent Stone.
- Ethereal Potion (priceless): Halts aging for a fixed period. Requires pixie dust, Grimbough leaves, Ebonspire bark, dragon heart.
- Rejuvenating Elixir (priceless): Complete healing of any wound. Ebonspire leaves.

Smithing: Crafts weapons and armor using Veyn to reinforce or enchant them during the forging process.
Blood-forging (using demon or angel blood in the forge) is one of the hardest skills in this class to learn, not every Smith can do it since demon and angel blood is reactive to Veyn, and a mistake can cause the death of the smith.

Rules & Notes

Rank and Role
A rank is a measure of power, not a profession. Every angel and demon has a rank — the amount of Veyn their body holds, shown by their wings and horns — but only some choose the army. Most angels and demons are civilians: merchants, craftsmen, scholars, laborers. Rank decides how strong you are. Role decides what you do.

The Ren Organ
Angels and demons are born to Veyn. But they can unlock a Ren Organ: a channel within the body that lets them draw on their own Vitality and use Ren. The cost is absolute — while the Ren Organ is open, the user cannot touch any form of Veyn at all. The moment Veyn and Ren meet inside the same body, the incompatibility rule applies and can kill them.

Rune Star Circles vs Grades
Mages do not use the Grade system — their power is measured by the Rune Star Circle inscribed into their heart. Comparison:
1st Circle (Initiate) ≈ F Grade
2nd Circle (Adept) ≈ E Grade
3rd Circle (Artificer) ≈ D Grade
4th Circle (Warden) ≈ C Grade
5th Circle (Archmage) ≈ B Grade
6th Circle (Sovereign) ≈ A Grade
7th Circle (Transcendent) ≈ S Grade

Cross-Class Skills
Every awakened person is assigned one class at the Sundering Altar. Humans can in theory learn the skills of any class, but in practice it is extremely difficult. The body's Veyn is shaped to its class at awakening, and forcing it to learn a foreign discipline is slow, painful, and usually fails. Most humans who try give up.

The Rarity of Generals
The generals of the Angel and Demon Realms — the Six Pillars and the Six Fingers — are so few that most people go their whole lives without ever seeing one. To the ordinary angel or demon, catching sight of a general is like a common citizen glimpsing the ruler of a country. Yet not every general is a hermit. A few take little strolls through their realm, walking among the people who would never otherwise see them.

Jaiden's Five Forms (detailed)

First Form (Normal): Sealed by the Auralis Ruby. Looks fully human. No wings, no horns, no halo. Lean young man, ~15, dark hair past ears, grey eyes. All 5 seals active.

Second Form (Halo & Horns): 1st seal released. A faint golden halo flickers above his head, two small horns curve from his forehead. Partial access to both angelic and demonic skills — weaker versions of each. The form of revelation.

Third Form (Full Angel): 2nd seal released. 6 pure white feathery wings, bright steady halo. Full access to Aetherblade Lord Angel skills. Identical to an Aetherblade Lord Angel.

Fourth Form (Full Demon): 3rd seal released. 6 horns and 6 dark purple feathery wings. Full access to Bloodward-level demonic skills.

Fifth Form (True Hybrid): 4th and 5th seals released. 3 pure white wings on one half, 3 black wings on the other, 6 horns, halo split in half. Both angelic and demonic auras surround him simultaneously. This is his true form.

The Rune Star System (Mage Progression)
The Rune Star is the foundation of all Mage power. Unlike other classes, Mages must physically inscribe Rune Star sigils into their heart. Each circle is a new sigil burned directly into the cardiac tissue by Veyn — the most sensitive organ in the body. The inscription process is excruciatingly painful. The rules are absolute: each circle must be fully completed before the next can be attempted. A half-inscribed Rune Star will destabilize and kill the Mage. Skipping circles causes instant death. The 7th Circle cannot be inscribed by the Mage themselves — it must be given by the world core. Only one being in recorded history has received this gift.

The Two Sundering Altars
There are two Sundering Altars. The Common Altar is in a neutral temple between the three realms, used by most ordinary people — it reveals only general affinity and rough grade estimate. The Academy Altar is within Elyndor Academy, reserved for students — it reveals exact class, precise starting grade, and innate abilities. Both were built by Kael von Ashen using Aelthar knowledge, but the Academy Altar was constructed with additional materials from the Hidden Realm.

Abilities: Channels both Holy Energy and Corrupted Veyn. 5 forms sealed by Auralis Ruby. In True Hybrid Form, skills fuse. Abnormally large Veyn reserves. Cannot take Vow, cannot make Pact, cannot mix Veyn and Ren.

Character Arc:
Act 1 (Books 1-3): Sealed boy arrives at Academy. First seal cracks during spar.
Act 2 (Books 4-9): Discovers hybrid nature. Learns truth from Kael. Masters forms. Public reveal at tournament.
Act 3 (Books 10-14): Fourth Turning. Sacrifices hybrid nature to mend Sundering on his own terms. Auralis Ruby shatters. Becomes the bridge between realms.

Key Relationships:
- Sofia Marlock: Guardian who raised him. Loves fiercely.
- Bella Rose: Childhood friend / love interest. Truth fractures then rebuilds.
- Mark Wesley: Best friend. Unconditional acceptance.
- Kael von Ashen: Mentor. Complicated agenda.
- Carin: Demonic trainer. Pushes hard.
- Licent Varak: Enemy. Killed his mother.
- Clint Ashborn: Antagonist. Fears him.
- Wener Leger: Manipulator. Uses truths.
- Tera Morage: Threat. Wants him dead.
- Ruby Divina: Protector/user. Shields as leverage.

surname Origin: Marlock is Sofia's surname. Jaiden uses it as his public identity.
