# Chapter 3 — Final Strategy

**Premise:** Jaiden is now at the Academy. The reader learns the world's magic and systems through him. **No guild system.**

---

## Core Delivery Method

Almost all information is delivered through:

1. A student asking a question
2. Another student answering (sometimes correctly, sometimes incompletely)
3. A teacher confirming, correcting, or expanding the answer
4. Or a teacher calling on a student to answer

This keeps everything natural and in-world. No lectures. No "Veyn 101" classes. Jaiden is a lazy, observant, slightly sarcastic teen — he absorbs the world by overhearing and half-listening, never by being spoon-fed.

---

## Chapter Structure (8 Beats)

### Beat 1 — The Formal Verdict
Jaiden is called in (or spoken to publicly) and the altar's reading is stated flatly:
- Class: **Mage**
- Grade: [number]
- **Anomaly flag noted.**

The cold judgment opens the chapter and sets the emotional tone. (This is the *Failure Frame* / *Shadow Slave* "verdict, not lecture" move — the system says what he is, and he feels it land.)

### Beat 2 — First Questions in the Hall / Courtyard
New students are talking. Someone asks what **Veyn** actually is. Another student answers: the silver-blue power that comes from the world itself. Someone asks about the red energy some fighters use — **Ren**, the power from inside the body — and that the two cannot exist together.

Jaiden mostly listens. The information arrives through conversation.

### Beat 3 — Watching Failure
Jaiden sees other students training and hitting their limits:
- A Mage overuses Veyn and collapses.
- An Archer runs dry.
- Someone using Ren burns out.

A student asks why people can't just learn everything. Another answers: humans *can* try, but almost no one succeeds because it's too hard. Other races are locked to what the altar gave them. *(This lands the "cross-class difficulty" fact we added — through a failure, not a rule.)*

### Beat 4 — The Grade Ceiling
A senior is pointed out as someone who reached a high grade and then stopped. A younger student asks why. The answer comes casually: most people hit a **Wall**. Progress stops. Some never move again. The Grade system is felt as a real ceiling, not a chart.

### Beat 5 — The Mage Path
In a small group or class setting, a teacher asks what makes Mage progression different. A student answers (or is corrected): Mages don't just rise in grade the normal way — they have to **carve progress into themselves** (Rune Stars). One mistake can cripple or kill.

The room goes quiet. Jaiden feels the weight of it. The cracked pendant throbs once. *(The emotional core — his path is self-inflicted surgery.)*

### Beat 6 — The Vow (Ritual)
From a distance, Jaiden watches **Thalor Elwin** oversee a young angel taking the **Veyn Vow**. He sees the halo form. He feels the second heartbeat stir. No one explains it to him. He simply understands he is shut out of it.

### Beat 7 — The Double Limit
A demon student complains about only being able to hold **five class skills**. A human student replies that humans could theoretically learn more, but almost nobody does. Jaiden realizes both of his true natures lock him out of the human flexibility while still subjecting him to non-human limits. He is restricted from both sides.

*(Corrected canon: Jaiden is angel + demon, no human half. So "learn everything" was never his, and even the five-cap barely applies to a being with "no rules.")*

### Beat 8 — Night Close
Dormitory. Quiet. The ruby pendant is still cracked and mending too slowly. The second heartbeat sounds once. Jaiden presses it down. The chapter ends on the personal cost, not on the systems.

## Voice & Style Rules
- First person, past tense
- Lazy, observant, slightly sarcastic teen voice
- **Thoughts in italics, no "I thought" tags**
- Information comes from other characters speaking — never from Jaiden suddenly knowing things
- New characters introduced by a known character with a "said" tag
- Short scenes, clear breaks (`*   *   *`), Times New Roman 12, bold chapter header

---

## What the Reader Should Know by the End of Chapter 3
- What Veyn is
- What Ren is
- Why they cannot mix
- How classes are assigned
- That grades exist and have a ceiling (the Wall)
- That Mages progress through dangerous internal carving (Rune Stars)
- The five-skill limit
- That humans have theoretical flexibility most never use
- That angels are bound by the Veyn Vow
- That Jaiden's reading is anomalous and being watched

## What Stays Hidden
- Guilds
- True hybrid nature
- World barrier / Aelthar / full history of the Headmaster
- Any advanced secrets (7th Rune Star, the world core's will, the curse)
